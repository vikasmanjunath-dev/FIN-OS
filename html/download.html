Download APK
This app is not on the Play Store. You install it straight from this page, so Android will ask you to allow it once. The steps below take about a minute.

Install in 5 steps

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download APK. If Chrome says “This type of file can harm your device”, tap Download anyway.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open on the notification, or open Files → Downloads and tap FIN-OS-1.0.0.apk.
  3. Android will say it can’t install apps from this source. Tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser (Chrome or Files).
  4. Go back and tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Tap Open. You can switch the “Install unknown apps” permission off again afterwards.

What works today

  • Works with no internet: net worth, budget and transaction log, goals, emergency fund, EPF / NPS / PPF, insurance, subscriptions, the Money Score, tax tools, retirement and loan planners, and statement import (read on your phone, never uploaded).
  • Not available yet for most people: live market data, stock quotes and Arya chat. They need the FIN-OS server, which isn’t open to the public yet. These screens will show as unavailable.
  • Optional sign-in lets you sync your trackers with the website. You can use the app without an account.
