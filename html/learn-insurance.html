System Logic 01

What is Insurance? (Risk Transfer)

Insurance is not an investment. It is a contract where you pay a small "Service Fee" (Premium) to a massive entity, so that when a "Critical Error" (Death, Accident, Fire) occurs, they bear the financial cost instead of you.

Brutal Truth: If you are buying insurance to "get money back," you are doing it wrong. That is a marketing trap.
LEVEL 02: HARDWARE PROTECTION

Health Insurance: The "Anti-Wipeout" Layer

In the Indian context, medical inflation is roughly 14%. A single bypass surgery or a 10-day ICU stay can delete years of compounding in a week.

🚫 No Room Rent Cap

If your policy has a cap (e.g., 1% of sum insured), and you take a better room, the company will cut your entire bill proportionally. It's a hidden kill-switch.

🚫 No Co-Pay

Co-pay means you pay a percentage of every bill. This is fine for small things, but catastrophic for a ₹20 Lakh bill.

LEVEL 03: LIFE PROTOCOL

Term Insurance: Pure Death Benefit

Most Indians buy "Endowment" plans (LIC style) because they "get money back." These plans give ~5% returns. Inflation is 6-7%. You are literally losing money to insure yourself.

The FIN-OS Formula

Cover = (Annual Income × 20) + Total Debt

If you earn ₹10L/year and have a ₹50L Home Loan, you need a ₹2.5 Crore Term Plan. Anything less is a system vulnerability.
