💡 Essential Real Estate Insights for Indian Buyers
🏦
EMI Rule of Thumb
Banks fund 75–80% of property value. Keep EMI ≤ 40% of take-home salary. CIBIL score 750+ gets best rates. Check your eligibility before shortlisting.
📋
RERA Compliance is Non-Negotiable
Verify on state RERA portal before booking. Builder must maintain escrow account (70% of collections). Always insist on possession date in writing.
💸
Hidden Costs Add 10–15%
Stamp duty + registration (5–8%), GST on under-construction (5–12%), society charges, broker fee (1–2%), furnishing, legal fees, and loan processing.
📊
LTCG Tax on Property Sale
After 2 years: 12.5% LTCG (indexation removed FY 2024). Section 54 gives exemption if you reinvest proceeds in another residential property within 2 years.
🏠
Real Rental Yields in India
Mumbai: 1.5–2.5%. Bengaluru: 2–3.5%. Pune: 2–3%. Net yield after maintenance, vacancy, and tax is usually 0.5–1% lower. Rarely beats equity long-term.
⏱️
Break-Even Is 7–12 Years
In most Indian cities, buying only beats renting after 7–12 years of holding. Shorter horizon? Rent + invest the difference almost always wins.
💡
Tax Benefits (Old Regime)
Section 24(b): ₹2L deduction on home loan interest. Section 80C: ₹1.5L on principal repayment (within 80C limit). Only applicable under Old Tax Regime.
⚡
The Opportunity Cost Question
₹20L down payment invested at 12% CAGR = ₹1.93 Cr in 20 years. Your property must appreciate beyond this to justify the illiquidity and EMI pressure.
