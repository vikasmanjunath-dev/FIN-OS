Commodity Protocol

When currency fails, rocks and oil survive.

LEVEL 01: NOOB

Real Assets

Commodities are raw materials. Oil, Gold, Silver, Wheat.

Unlike stocks, they don't produce earnings. A bar of gold will remain one bar of gold forever. It won't give birth to a baby gold bar (Dividend).

🏭 What Moves a Commodity?

Commodity prices can reflect supply, demand, inventories, weather, transport, currency movements, and geopolitical events. Unlike a productive company, a physical commodity may not generate cash flows while you hold it.

LEVEL 02: MECHANIC

The "Jewelry" Trap

👑 Investment vs Ornament

Jewelry: Making charges, taxes, and resale deductions affect the full cost of jewellery. Compare the invoice and likely resale terms; jewellery can have personal value as well as a commodity value.

Gold ETFs and app-based digital gold: Different products with different risks. ETFs are securities regulated by SEBI; app-based digital gold is outside SEBI’s securities-market framework and carries counterparty and operational risks. Read SEBI’s caution.

💍 Ownership Is Not the Same

Jewellery combines metal value with making charges, design, and retail margins. An investment product may track a reference price but has its own fees, liquidity, issuer, and tax treatment. Compare what you own and how you can exit.

LEVEL 03: PRO

SGB (Sovereign Gold Bond)

A Sovereign Gold Bond is a government security linked to gold prices and carries the terms set out in its issue notification. Its market price can move, and the bond may trade at a premium or discount.

SGBs are issued on behalf of the Government of India. They avoid physical storage, but carry price, liquidity, and issuer-term considerations. Check the specific issue’s redemption and current tax rules before investing.

📜 Read the Instrument

For any government-backed or exchange-traded product, verify current issuance or trading availability, eligibility, redemption terms, secondary-market liquidity, and tax rules. Product names that mention the same commodity can represent very different claims.

☠️ GOD MODE: The Hedge

Gold can behave differently from equities, but the relationship changes over time and is not reliably negative.

When Stock Market crashes (fear), Gold usually shoots up. A gold allocation may diversify a portfolio, but its size and effect depend on the assets, period, and investor. It can also fall in value.

✅ Hedge Check

A hedge is useful only if it offsets a risk you actually have. Consider whether the instrument tracks that exposure, how much it can move, and what it costs to hold. A hedge can lose value or fail to offset the underlying risk.
