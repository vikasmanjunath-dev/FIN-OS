How this is calculated — and what it can't tell you
  • Both strategies spend the same cash every month (EMI + your extra). Prepay puts the extra into the loan, so it closes early and the whole EMI + extra is invested afterwards. Invest keeps the loan on its normal schedule and invests the extra from day one. Wealth is compared when the loan would have ended.
  • Prepaying is a guaranteed return equal to your loan rate (after tax relief, if you claim 24(b)). Investment returns are an assumption — equities can be down for years. The break-even figure is the return you must reliably beat.
  • Gains tax is charged once at the end: equity LTCG 12.5% above the ₹1.25L exemption, debt/FD gains at your slab, plus 4% cess. Returns compound monthly at the annual rate ÷ 12, like every FIN-OS calculator.
  • Sec 24(b) relief is modelled as a monthly tax saving on interest up to ₹2L a year, and is invested too. It is not available under the new tax regime.
  • Not modelled: prepayment charges (none on floating-rate loans per RBI), rate resets, changes in your income, and the value of liquidity — cash in a loan is hard to get back; cash in a fund is not. Keep an emergency fund before doing either. This is a planning aid, not investment advice.
