What you are judging. Each card shows one sentence from an answer the engine gave (the claim) next to the passages it was given (the sources). Decide whether the claim is supported by those passages.

  • Supported (key 1 or S): the passages state it, or it is a plain restatement of what they state. Numbers must match.
  • Not supported (key 2 or N): the passages contradict it, or do not state it (an invented detail, a number taken from a different fact, a rule stretched beyond what the text says).
  • Unsure (key 3 or U): you cannot tell. These are not counted toward the 300.

Judge only against the passages shown, not against what you know to be true in general. Nothing is pre-filled. Backspace or Z undoes the last label, ← / → move without labelling. Progress is saved in this browser after every click; use Download labels to keep a file.
