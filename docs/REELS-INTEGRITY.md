# Money Reels: cross-slice integrity review (Oct 24 2026)

A review of everything the parallel sessions built for the reels (player performance refactor, journey/profile, ranker v3, follow-up, Your Number, web feed, accessibility, share image, author tooling, trust notes, cold start, transparency, notifications v2, challenges v2, Hindi/compliance sheets, Arya bridge, Home card). Each session had verified only its own slice. This pass read the seams between them.

**Result:** 13 defects fixed (1 high, 6 medium, 6 low), each with a regression test in `mobile/__tests__/reels-integrity.test.mts` (18 tests, which also hold the drift guards below); 13 items listed for owners (3 medium, 9 low, 1 info). After the fixes: `npm test` 1,585 pass, `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run lint` clean, `export-reels-web.mts --check` says the website data is current, and the site's own `node --test tests/*.test.js` passes (546). Checked in the web preview (`/learn/reels`, `/dashboard`): the feed plays to its question, labels read as intended, Home's card and the feed name the same reel, answering twice counts once. Nothing was checked on a phone (notifications, TalkBack/VoiceOver, native share).

Severity: **high** = a privacy claim or a safety guard is wrong in practice; **medium** = a real defect a person can meet; **low** = edge case, latent, copy or consistency.

## Checked and fine

- **Storage keys:** every key the reels write is in `lib/privacy.ts` and starts with `finos_session_` except `finos_reels_watched` and `finos_reels_days`, which are declared as syncing; no other writer of `finos_session_reels_profile` than the journey hook and the transparency resets; no `fetch`, `console`, clipboard or `Share.share` of personal data anywhere in the reels code; the share card, share text, web JSON and Arya route carry library ids and example figures only.
- **Parsers:** every new optional profile field (`start`, `tips`, `blindWeek`, `fmt`, `saved`, `pledges`, `ms`, `hadLoan`) is validated and bounded (60 saved, 20 pledges, 200 known, 90 stat days, 10 open moves, 20 receipts, 10 move reminders, 40 follow-up records); stored lists cannot grow without limit.
- **Dates:** seasons (Diwali 2026-2030, Budget week, March), the weekly window, weekKey, the nudge window and quiet hours, the weekend and month-start options, the streak across midnight: no off-by-one found. The website's seasons, fact of the day, weekly line, sliders, beats and saved lists have parity tests against the TypeScript.
- **Player:** pause/resume, jump, replay, voice hold at a scene's end, the beat pause, sound toggling (position remapped), backgrounding and timers on unmount are correct; the only defect was the silent last tick above.
- **Guards:** the distress guard holds in the ranker, the first episode and its bias, follow-up, blind spot, the Arya chip, the nudge titles and (now) the banners and personalised reels. Arya's chip is silent on refused, flagged and advice questions.
- **Packaging:** no import cycles; no `scripts/` or Node imports from app code; `@/` aliases resolve; `react-native-view-shot` and `expo-sharing` are required lazily.
