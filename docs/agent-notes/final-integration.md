## Defects fixed
1. **Safe areas**: `react-native-safe-area-context` 5.4.0 is installed (dependency of expo-router; nothing added). `app/_layout.tsx` wraps the app in `SafeAreaProvider` and pads the top by the inset (screens, Back links, lock screen, error screen). `app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` tab bar height/padding use the bottom inset (same 72 as before when the inset is 12 or less). `TrackerScreen` adds the bottom inset to its scroll padding. iOS modal sheets are placed by the system and get no extra top padding. **Needs a device.**
2. **Health score from nothing**: `lib/trackers.ts` no longer uses the neutral DNA baseline (8 points) or the 60,000 income fallback. Each of the 7 areas has `hasData`; an area with nothing recorded adds no points and is left out of the total, which is scaled to 100 over the recorded areas (`total`, `tier`, `measured`, `missing`, `points`, `maxPoints`). Fewer than 2 recorded areas: `total` is `null` and Home / Health screen say "Not enough data"; `persistDerived` writes no `finos_health_score` / `_detail` and removes a stale one. Knowledge counts only when a lesson is marked learned (the streak is the app being opened, not a recorded fact). Callers updated (Home, Health screen, Arya context and tool, PULSE card, report, `useFinosContext`). Tests: logic (3), learn, dna.parity, aryaContext updated; mutation not re-run. Deliberately different from the website now (the old "300 scenarios identical" claim is annotated in MOBILE_APP.md).
3. `TrackerScreen` title has `accessibilityRole="header"`.
4. Dark purple text: `Colors.purpleInk` for text in `tools/offers`, `tools/prepay` and the `TwoLineChart` legend (`components/CalcUI.tsx`); fills unchanged. Not re-measured in dark (theme-audit is light only).

6. Titles: `TrackerScreen` title uses `flexShrink`, `numberOfLines={3}`, `adjustsFontSizeToFit`, `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1.4}`; Back link min height 44. **The web preview still breaks "Benchmarks" at the audit's 2x CSS scaling** (it ignores these props); native behaviour unverified.
