# Cold start: the first 60 seconds

Part of the Money Reels guide (`docs/REELS.md`, section 4).

## The welcome, the nudge and the first episode

A new person used to get the generic ranked episode with no say in it. Now they meet Mitra, tap up to three answers (or Skip), play a short first episode and reach the calm stop. Never a quiz first. Code: `lib/reels/coldstart.ts` (pure), `components/reels/Welcome.tsx`, a new optional `start` field in the reels profile (`reelsProfile.ts`), and a surgical wiring in `app/learn/reels.tsx` and `hooks/useReelsJourney.ts`. Tests: `__tests__/reels-coldstart.test.mts`.

- **The welcome.** One calm full-screen sheet, shown **once**: when the profile has no `start` and no reel has been finished (`needsWelcome`). A link to a single reel (`/learn/reels?id=...`) skips it. Questions, each one tap, each skippable: (1) language, English or हिन्दी, **only when the app has no language stored yet** (`useLangUnset`; asked first, because the questions after it are read in it); (2) "What are you here for?" (Start saving or investing, Handle a loan or EMI, Stay safe from scams, Understand tax, Just curious); (3) "How comfortable are you with money terms?" (New to this, I know some, Comfortable). **Skip** is always visible and is remembered (`start.skipped`), so the welcome never asks again; there is a Back link, 44 px+ tap targets, large text up to 1.5-1.6x, labels for screen readers, and a line saying the answers stay on this phone and can be changed.
- **What is stored.** `profile.start = { goals: Topic[]; level: 'new'|'some'|'comfortable'; at: string; skipped?: true }` in `finos_session_reels_profile` (already in the privacy inventory, label updated; never synced). `parseStart` keeps only known topics (at most three), a known level (else `some`) and a real date; anything else and the welcome simply asks again. "Just curious" and Skip store no topics.
- **The first-week nudge** (`firstEpisodeBias`, `applyBias`). From the day of the answers, a chosen topic moves up the ranker's list, and the ladder level tilts it (New: myths and concepts earlier, decisions and actions later; Comfortable: the reverse; "I know some": no change). It shrinks evenly each day and is **exactly nothing from day 7** (`BIAS_DAYS`), also for Skip or an unreadable date. It only reorders fresh reels, and only among the places fresh reels already held: a missed question, a refresher, a pledge, a timely or gentle pick keep their exact place. It cannot add a reel the ranker removed, and for a person under strain it also drops growth reels. `ranker.ts` is **not** changed; the feed applies the nudge to the ranker's output. If you want it inside the ranker, call `firstEpisodeBias(profile.start, now)` there and add `bias.topics[topic] + bias.levels[level]` (in places of rank, not points) to fresh reels only.
- **The first-ever episode** (`firstEpisode`). On the first day with the reels (everything finished so far was finished today: `isFirstDay`), the feed uses this instead of `composeEpisode`: exactly **two reels**, a playable **myth** reel on the chosen topic (it has a usable beat), then a **concept** reel (on the same topic when there is one), then the calm stop with a "Tomorrow:" teaser. Fallbacks, in order: a playable concept or decision on the chosen topic; a playable myth anywhere; any playable reel; the ranker's top reel. With nothing chosen the ranker's order decides. Growth reels ("put in more") are never picked for a person under strain, even if they chose investing. If one reel was finished and the person returns the same day, only the second is offered; after two the screen is the calm stop alone ("Keep exploring" is still there). The weekly blind spot and the "Still on your list" pledge are not used in the first episode.
- **Changing answers later.** The calm stop has a quiet 44 px row under it, "Change my answers" (or "Tell Mitra what you want to learn" after a Skip), drawn by `app/learn/reels.tsx` around `EpisodeEnd` (which is unchanged). It reopens the welcome with the earlier answers shown as chosen; Skip there only closes it. New answers start a new 7 days of nudge and apply from the next visit (today's episode stays fixed). Any other screen can reopen it the same way: the welcome is `<Welcome visible initial={profile.start} onDone={start => j.chooseStart(start)} .../>` and `chooseStart` is returned by `useReelsJourney`. `changeLabel(start)` gives the row's text in both languages.
- **Hindi** for every string is in `coldstart.ts` (`COPY`, `GOAL_OPTIONS`, `LEVEL_OPTIONS`, ...), a test checks each has Devanagari. It is a draft for a native reviewer, like the rest.
- **Not checked on a phone:** VoiceOver/TalkBack order on the welcome, the iOS/Android modal behaviour, and how the sheet looks at 2x text on a 320 px screen.
