# Reminders, nudges and notification taps

Part of the Money Reels guide (`docs/REELS.md`, section 5). All local: no server, no background task. Nothing here has run on a real phone.

## When will you do it? and the daily nudge

- **When will you do it?** On the answer screen, "Remind me when I'll do it" (for reels with a tool) opens `WhenSheet`: tonight 8 pm (only while it is still ahead), tomorrow 9 am, Saturday 10 am, the start of next month 9 am (`whenOptions` in `lib/reels/reminders.ts`). Picking one asks the phone for notification permission once, keeps one local reminder for that reel (`finos_session_reels_reminders`), starts a move so a receipt can follow, and schedules it. The lock screen shows only "Your money move" and the tool's name, never an amount.
- **Daily nudge.** The bell in the reels top bar opens `NudgeSheet`: off until turned on, an hour from 8, 9, 12, 18 or 20 (`NUDGE_HOURS`, never before 7 or after 21), one a day for the next 3 days, naming the public title of a lesson the next episode would offer. A day on which a reel was already finished gets none, and every app start and return to the app re-plans, so it stays silent once the day is done. No streak or guilt wording (a test checks the copy). Stored in `finos_session_reels_nudge`.
- **Plumbing.** Pure planning in `lib/reels/reminders.ts`; OS calls in `lib/reels/reminders-native.ts` (ids start `finosreel-`, so the calendar reminders in `lib/remindersNative.ts`, ids `finos-`, never cancel them); choices and the sync in `lib/reelRemindersStorage.ts`, called from `app/_layout.tsx` at start and on foreground. Notices follow the app language. **Tapping a notice** opens its screen: `lib/notificationTaps.ts` reads `data.route` from a plain tap (not a dismissal or an action button), accepts only a plain in-app path (`ROUTE`: slash-separated words, no scheme, host, dots or query), handles each notice once, and also handles the tap that launched the app; `app/_layout.tsx` starts it and retries briefly while the navigator mounts. It covers the calendar reminders too. Native scheduling and tap handling are untested on a real device (the web preview shows "Reminders work in the phone app"); `__tests__/notification-taps.test.mts` drives it with a fake notifications module.

## Notifications v2: follow-up, Sunday look-back, one merged plan

Everything is local; no server and no background task. Rules that hold for every notice the reels schedule: at most **one a day across everything**, only between **8:00 and 21:00** local, copy written as curiosity (no fear, urgency, FOMO or guilt, nothing about what was missed), and **no amount or personal figure on the lock screen** (only fixed words and a lesson's public title). Tests: `mobile/__tests__/reels-notify2.test.mts`.

- **`planAll(state, now)`** (`lib/reels/reminders.ts`) is the single pure scheduler. Inputs: nudge prefs, notify prefs, the person's move reminders, pledges still waiting, the on-phone stats, `doneToday`, strain, the lesson titles, language. Output: at most 6 notices (`MAX_SCHEDULED`), sorted by time, 14-day horizon (`HORIZON_DAYS`). Who wins a day: the person's own "when will you do it?" reminder, then a pledge follow-up, then the Sunday look-back, then the daily nudge. A reminder or follow-up that loses its day moves to the next free day at the same time (never silently dropped); the look-back and nudge simply skip it. The cap also keeps the person's own reminders first. The daily nudge still looks only `NUDGE_DAYS` (3) ahead, and a reminder the person set is kept however far off (e.g. "start of next month"), so it does not need the app to be reopened. A day a reel was finished is silent for the nudge, follow-up and look-back; a reminder the person asked for at a time they chose is still delivered. The old five-argument call shape still works and is `planAllClassic`.
- **Pledge follow-up.** A pledge ("I'll do this", `profile.pledges`) whose reel's tool was not started (`startedReelIds`), with no reminder set for it and not marked "I know this", gets one notice on **day 3 at the person's nudge hour** (18:00 until they pick): title "A small step on your list", body "Still thinking about "<the reel's public title>"? 2 minutes." (Hindi draft in the same file). A title that carries an amount, a percentage or a frightening word (`unfitForLockScreen`) is replaced by "that money step". Tap opens the reel's tool route (`ACT_ALL[id].href`), else `/learn/reels`. **Max one per pledge**: the time it is placed at is recorded in `fu` (`finos_session_reels_notify`); a recorded time that has passed means sent, never again; a pledge not seen by day 3 is still followed up once at the next hour that is ahead, and one older than 14 days is let go. It stops when the move is started, a reminder is set, the pledge is removed (its record is pruned on the next sync), or the person is under money strain (as in `followup.ts`).
- **Sunday look-back.** Opt-in, **default off**. Sunday 19:00, only if `weekLine(weekOf(stats, thatSunday))` is non-null, i.e. there were reels in the seven days ending that Sunday as far as is known when planning (a week with no reels gets nothing). Title "Your week in reels", body "A quiet look back, about 30 seconds." The notice carries no figures; the figures are in the weekly line shown on the calm stop. **Limit:** a notice cannot carry a deep link with a query (`ROUTE` forbids it), so a tap opens `/learn/reels`, the feed, which shows the calm stop (and the weekly line) once today's episode is done. Making the tap land on the calm stop directly needs a route parameter in `app/learn/reels.tsx`, which this change did not touch.
- **Milestones are not notified.** A real milestone is noticed from the figures already on the phone, and noticing them needs the app to run (`checkMilestones`). Noticing one while the app is in the background would need a background task, which this app deliberately does not use. So **the gold milestone banner in the feed is the only place a milestone appears**; there is no milestone notification. If a background task is ever added, it needs its own consent and this section updated.
- **Season-aware nudge.** `planNudges` picks the headline by `seasonOf` of the nudge's own day: Diwali week "Diwali week: a calm money thought", Budget week "Budget week: a 30-second lesson", March year-end "March year-end: one small lesson" (EN and HI), otherwise "A 30-second money lesson". The body is still a lesson's public title.
- **Settings** (`NudgeSheet`, the bell in the reels top bar, now titled "Reminders"): the daily switch and hour, **"Follow-ups on things I said I would do"** and **"Sunday look-back"**, and a **"Turn all reminders off"** row (nudge, follow-ups, look-back, and the reminders set after a reel; cancels every `finosreel-` notice). Follow-ups default **on only after the person set a daily nudge or a first reminder** (`followupsOn`; the sync stores `armed` once it sees either, so it stays on if the nudge is later switched off), otherwise off; an explicit choice always wins. Turning a switch on asks the phone for permission once, like the daily one. Switches carry screen-reader labels and hints; rows and hour buttons are at least 44 px; the card scrolls on small screens.
- **Storage and sync.** New key `finos_session_reels_notify` (`{ followups: boolean | null, weekly, armed, fu }`, normalised on load, never synced, in the person's export and backup, listed in `lib/privacy.ts`). `syncReelReminders` re-plans from today's data and **cancels and re-adds only ids starting `finosreel-`**; calendar reminders (`finos-`) are never touched. Re-planning with the same data gives the same plan (a test applies it twice). The transparency list (`lib/reels/transparency.ts`, another change's file) already names this key under reminders.
- **Taps** (`lib/notificationTaps.ts`): notices now carry `data.kind` (`move`, `nudge`, `followup`, `weekly`). The plain-path rule is unchanged for every notice; additionally `KIND_ROUTES` pins the look-back and the nudge to `/learn/reels`, and a non-text `kind` is refused. A follow-up or move opens whatever tool screen it links to, as before.
- **Needs a real phone:** scheduling, the permission prompt, Android channel behaviour, delivery at the planned minute (the plan stays at 6 or fewer, well under iOS's 64 scheduled-notification limit), and the tap landing. The web preview shows "Reminders work in the phone app".

## Remind me on My moves

- **A gentle weekly line** at the calm stop (`lib/reels/weekly.ts`): the last seven days (today included) added up from the on-phone health counters, for example "This week: 9 reels on 4 days, 2 moves on your list, 1 move started." It says only what happened, is silent in a week with no reels, and never mentions what was missed (a test checks the wording). It refreshes whenever a reel is finished.
- **"⏰ Remind me" on My moves.** The "when will you do it?" logic moved out of the feed into `hooks/useMoveReminder.ts`, used by both the feed and My moves; a test checks neither keeps its own copy. On the web preview picking a time says reminders run in the phone app only, as before.
