# What the reels know about you

Part of the Money Reels guide (`docs/REELS.md`, section 6).

## The screen

One screen, `/learn/reels-data` ("What the reels know about you"), shows in plain words everything the reels keep on this phone, why today's first reel was picked, and a reset for each part. Learn → a card; Diagnostics → a row next to Reels health; reachable by route from anywhere else.

- **Files.** `mobile/lib/reels/transparency.ts` (pure: the summary in both languages, what each reset does, `whyThisReel`), `mobile/lib/reels/transparencyStorage.ts` (loads everything, `resetPart(id)`, `resetAllReels()`), `mobile/app/learn/reels-data.tsx` (the screen), tests in `mobile/__tests__/reels-transparency.test.mts`.
- **Parts**, each with a summary, detail lines (reel titles, topics, statuses; at most 8 with "and N more"), where it syncs, and its own reset: reels you know, topics you asked for less of, reels saved, moves on your list, real changes noticed (milestones), what you told the welcome, how you play (the interactive parts, said as "you like to try things", never as a rate), moves being watched and receipts, reels finished (and the streak days), daily counts, challenges, reminders (daily nudge, follow-ups, Sunday look-back, reminders set after a reel), voice choices, small housekeeping (gesture tips, the week of the weekly look). Below them: the figures read from the trackers (SIP, spending, fund, loan) with a note that they are never sent or copied, so there is nothing to reset; "Reset everything about reels"; the honest note about what never leaves the phone.
- **What a reset does.** It only removes the reels' own memory. Per-field resets are the `forget*` helpers at the end of `reelsProfile.ts` (pure; they return the same object when there is nothing to forget, so no write happens); `resetMoves`, `resetChallenges`, `resetWatched`, `resetVoicePrefs`, `resetReelReminders` (also cancels the `finosreel-` notifications; the phone's notification permission is untouched) and the existing `clearStats` are the others. The person's trackers are never written. Confirmation is `window.confirm` on the web and `Alert.alert` on a phone, saying what is and is not touched.
- **Resetting "moves" changes how the list reads:** a pledge's status line ("watching your figure") comes from the watched figures, so after that reset it says "not started". Resetting milestones makes the next check take a fresh silent baseline, so nothing already true is celebrated again.
- **Sync, honestly.** Every reels key except `finos_reels_watched` and `finos_reels_days` starts with `finos_session_` and is never uploaded. Those two sync with the other tracker data if the person signs in, so resetting "reels finished" removes the phone copy only; the screen says the cloud copy can bring it back until it is deleted in Privacy & your data.
- **Why this reel.** `whyThisReel(reel, ranked)` turns the ranker's `why` (review, refresh, timely, gentle, blind, pillar, new, pledge, done) and its reason into one plain sentence in both languages ("because you saved it", "because it is Diwali week", "because your Savings is your lowest Money Score pillar", "because you missed this question last time"). The saved and season sentences are recognised by the ranker's own wording; a test asks the real ranker for each and fails if the wording changes. It never shows a figure of the person's.
- **A key cannot be forgotten.** Tests derive the reels keys from `DATA_ITEMS` in `lib/privacy.ts` and fail naming any that is not in `PART_KEYS`, and scan the app code for `finos_(session_)reels_*` literals that are not in the registry. When someone adds a reels key they add it to the registry, to `PART_KEYS` and to a reset. Adding a field to `ReelsProfile` also needs a line here: the welcome answers (`start`) were added by another change and caught the same way, by reading the profile.
- **Not verified.** The Hindi is a draft for a native reviewer. Checked in the web preview (real stored values, a reset removing only its field, 375 px width with no horizontal scroll, 44 px targets, Hindi) but not with VoiceOver or TalkBack, not at 2x text size on a device, and not the native `Alert` (the web uses `window.confirm`).
