# Recording brief: reel voice (English, Indian accent)

How to read this. One row per line the viewer hears. Record each line as its own clip, named by the Clip column. Keep to the Length window: the picture and the caption are timed to it.

- **Voice**: a warm, clear narrator, like a friendly older sibling who knows money. Never a salesperson. Never urgent unless the Feeling says so.
- **Numbers**: say them as written in the Say column (rupees, percent). Say "1930" digit by digit. Say acronyms letter by letter (E M I, S I P) unless the Say column spells a word.
- **No advice voice**: every figure is an example or an assumption. Do not sound certain about a return.
- **Timing**: each clip starts about 180 ms after its caption appears and ends about 300 ms before the next scene. Leave no long silence at the start or end of a clip.
- **Delivery**: 48 kHz, mono, WAV, peaking about -3 dB; one file per clip. Do not add music.
